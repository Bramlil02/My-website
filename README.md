Project overview 
The purpose of the website to promote exposure and helping nicro improve by getting exposure and support to reduce the crime rate in soucthafrica

Website Goals And Objectives
-To attract people to come support the organisation
- Nicro helps ex-convicts and convicts to get their lifes back on track by educating them to go to school and get jobs so the can support their family and they also influence children to make right decision and to stay in schools
- Nicro measure the effectiveness of its crime prevention and offender reintegration programs, as well as the organizations operational and financial heealth.

  Key Features and Functionality
  -Responsive navigation menu
  -About us section about the organisations history goals and vision,mission.
  -Service page the information about the services given by the organisation
  -Functional Enquiry form
  -Contact page with a map and contact details and form
