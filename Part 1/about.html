About Nicro

Nicro was founded in September 1910, Nicro is one of the largest most enduring South Africa non-profit organisations. Nicro specializes in social crime prevention and offender reintegration for adults and children. Nicro has a rich history in human rights, Juvenile justice and innovative criminal justice reform. Nicro has offices in seven of the nine provinces, and renders services to approximately 12000 to 15000 direct beneficiaries and touches the lives of between 40000 to 50000 South Africans each year.

VISION

    -The vision Nicro has is a much safer SouthAfrica without crime and violence.

MISSION

    -Their mission is to lead the way to ending crime and building safe communities.
